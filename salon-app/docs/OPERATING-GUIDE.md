# A simple daily guide

## Manager: start the day

1. Sign in as Salon manager on the live app address.
2. Click Refresh and check the connection status.
3. Check appointments, low stock and customer dues.
4. Save staff attendance and leave status.
5. Check that service prices and stock are ready for billing.

## Make a bill

1. Open New bill.
2. Select an existing customer, or add one. Use Walk-in only when no unpaid balance will remain.
3. Click the service or retail product. Adjust the quantity if needed.
4. Select the team member for each line so sales and incentives are assigned correctly.
5. Enter any approved discount in rupees and its reason.
6. Record money already received. Use All cash, All UPI or All card for a full payment, or fill different amounts for a split payment.
7. Save bill & payment. Wait for the saved receipt.
8. Print it or use the browser's Save as PDF option.

Stock is reduced only after a successful saved bill. Service stock is deducted only when the owner has entered the service's consumption list. Do not click a payment shortcut unless money was actually received.

## Collect an old due

Open Invoices & dues → find the bill → View → Receive payment. Enter the amount, method and reference. The payment is recorded on today's date while the original invoice date stays unchanged.

## Receive stock

Open Inventory → Receive, or Expenses & purchases → Stock purchases → Receive stock. Record one product per entry. Enter the supplier bill number, quantity, unit cost, total and amount paid. Attach a small receipt if available. Unpaid balances appear as supplier dues and can be paid later.

For a stock count difference, use Adjust and enter a clear reason. A positive number adds stock; a negative number removes stock. Never use an adjustment to hide a missing purchase or sale.

## Care for clients

Keep customer preferences, service feedback and agreed follow-up notes in the customer record. Record promotional consent before making marketing calls. Use the visit history to discuss the next appointment. Membership dates are reminders; any discount must still be entered on the bill.

## End the day

Save all bills, payments, purchases, expenses and attendance. Open Day closing, enter opening cash, any extra cash added or taken out, and the actual cash counted. Explain any difference. Close today. The owner can see the closing and all reports after refresh.

The reminders on the closing screen are local checkboxes; the financial closing is the saved record. The app does not automatically message the owner.

## Owner: daily review

Open Reports, choose a date range, then check sales, collections, expenses, dues, staff incentives and stock planning. Check the Change history for unusual adjustments, discounts or cancellations. Download a CSV or print the selected report as PDF.

Use Settings to update the business details and manager discount limit. Use Services & prices and Inventory to control prices and product records. A saved bill preserves its original details even when prices change later.

## Correct mistakes

Bills are not silently edited or deleted. The owner can cancel a bill with a reason and record any money actually refunded. Used service products should not be added back to stock. Only choose stock return if all recorded consumed items are genuinely unused and returned.

If an expense or purchase was entered incorrectly, retain the original receipt and contact the owner/developer for an audited correction. This version has no expense/purchase reversal screen. Do not make a second entry that hides the first one.
